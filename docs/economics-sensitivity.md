# Economics and break-even sensitivity (assumptions only)

> **Status:** ASSUMPTIONS ONLY. No invoices, no field data, no measured loss prevention.
> Generated by `scripts/economics.py` from `backend/app/ops/economics.py`. Every number is per 1,000 cash-outs in BDT unless stated. Nothing here is a measured result.

## What this model separates

1. **Cost** of running the workflow: placed call attempts (failed ones included), SMS, manual review, case review with an independent follow-up, hosting.
2. **Detection**: a call can surface a customer-reported mismatch. A flagged cash gap or a closed case is **not** a saved taka and is never counted as one.
3. **Benefit**: only what a separate, supported intervention returns (`intervention_success_rate`). The primary cash-out has already completed when the call is made, so prevention needs a hold or reversal that this workflow does not provide on its own. With rate 0 the net is the negative of the cost.

## Break-even

- Intervention returns 10pct: break-even average loss per incident ≈ ৳2,081
- Intervention returns 25pct: break-even average loss per incident ≈ ৳832
- Intervention returns 50pct: break-even average loss per incident ≈ ৳416
- At the simulated loss of ৳61 per incident the intervention share needed to break even is above 100%: no intervention rate pays for an all-call workflow.

Reading the table: at a lower answer rate the net can look *less* negative (for example 40% versus 60% at 0% intervention) only because fewer answered calls create fewer cases and follow-ups to staff. That is cost avoided by not reaching customers, not a benefit.

## Not counted anywhere

- flagged cash gaps
- closed cases
- supervisor-confirmed findings without restitution
- deterrence
- customer trust
- regulatory value

## What would replace these assumptions

Real invoices (call and SMS price per attempt, billed unanswered fraction), measured answer and completion rates, measured manual handling time, confirmed-finding and restitution counts from a governed pilot, and a defined intervention with its own success rate.
